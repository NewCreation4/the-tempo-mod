   Tanks
# [T1] Light Tank: Glony-296
- Cheap, fast to produce
- Ok range and damage
- Lightly armored (10), 150 health
- Fastest movement speed and turret turn speed
- Immune to the wet status effect, amphibious

# [T2] Medium Tank: Makrofagi-23-08
- Moderate cost and production speed
- Better range (still gets hit by Anti) and damage, low armor piercing
+ Has an autocannon for lightly armored ground threats (less range than main gun, automatically fires)
- Moderately armored (25), 300 health
- Somewhat fast movement and turret turning

# [T3] Battle tank
- Moderate cost and production speed (slightly higher than medium tank though)
- Outranges Anti, good damage and moderate armor piercing
+ Has an autocannon for lightly armored ground threats (equal range as the main gun, automatically fires)
- Decent armor (30), 450 health
- Somewhat fast movement and turret turning

# [T3] Heavy tank 
- Expensive, slow to produce
- Outranges Anti, lots of damage and moderate armor piercing
- Heavy armor (40), 450 health
- Very slow movement and turret turning
